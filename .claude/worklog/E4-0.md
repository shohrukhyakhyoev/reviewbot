## Decisions pending (ADR candidates)
- R1 fix (Claude, 2026-11-20, shape chosen by the human: option a): generation fencing. Each `register` gets a fresh generation (global `AtomicLong`); the poller stamps every record at dispatch with `tracker.track(tp, offset)` → `TrackedOffset(partition, generation, offset)`; the drainer hands it back via `markComplete(TrackedOffset)`, which counts only when the generation matches the registered state, otherwise permit only. `track` on an unregistered partition throws `IllegalStateException` (dispatch bug). Deviates from the §8.4 sketch signature `markComplete(tp, off)`; CLAUDE.md I7 requires generation fencing. For PROGRESS key decisions at close: E4-3 calls `track` per record at dispatch and carries the `TrackedOffset` to the drainer; E4-2 passes it to `markComplete` unchanged.
- Property-test approach (ticket's conditional ADR): Claude used seeded `Random` over 500 JUnit `@ParameterizedTest` seeds (no new dependency, failing seed reproduces) instead of jqwik (shrinking, but a new parent-POM pin). Human to confirm and write the ADR.
- Decided (R3, Claude at the human's request, 2026-11-20): follow the spec (§8.4 O5 "since last commit"). `committable()` is a pure read of watermarks advanced since the last CONFIRMED commit; new `markCommitted(map)` (same watermarks map, not +1; keeps the max) is called by E4-3's poller in the `commitAsync` success callback and after `commitSync`. An unconfirmed commit is reported again, incl. at revocation/shutdown. No spec deviation left.
